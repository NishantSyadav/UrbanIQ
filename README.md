🏙️ **UrbanIQ** – AI-Powered Civic Intelligence Platform

> AI-powered civic intelligence platform built for the **Community Hero – Hyperlocal Problem Solver** hackathon challenge.

🚀 **Overview**
UrbanIQ empowers citizens and municipal authorities to collaboratively report, verify, prioritize, track, and resolve civic issues using multimodal AI and an autonomous agentic engine called **Nebula**.

🎯 **Problem:**
Communities struggle with fragmented reporting, duplicate complaints, poor prioritization, and lack of transparency.

UrbanIQ transforms scattered reports into actionable civic intelligence.

 🌌 **Nebula – Autonomous Agentic AI Orchestrator**

Nebula coordinates multiple AI capabilities instead of acting as a single chatbot.

**Responsibilities:**
- Analyze uploaded images
- Analyze uploaded videos
- Understand citizen descriptions
- Categorize issues
- Estimate severity
- Detect duplicates
- Recommend responsible departments
- Generate summaries
- Update hotspot analytics
- Contribute to predictive risk zones
